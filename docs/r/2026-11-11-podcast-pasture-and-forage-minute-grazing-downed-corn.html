Pasture and Forage Minute: Grazing Downed Corn

Released 2026-11-11

With corn harvest wrapping up, some fields have more grain left behind than normal. Stalk rots, wind damage, and lodging can leave dropped ears or missed corn that cattle will find quickly. Too much grain at once, though, can cause acidosis, or grain overload. Ben Beckman, Nebraska Extension Forage
