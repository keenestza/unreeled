Will Ospreay Reveals How Much Pain He Was In Prior To Neck Surgery, & More

Released 2026-09-11

During a recent appearance on the “Insight with Chris Van Vliet” podcast, reigning AEW World Champion Will Ospreay opened up about the neck injury that sidelined him for several months. Ospreay suffered herniated discs in his C-2 and C-6 vertebrae, ultimately requiring surgery to repair the damage.
