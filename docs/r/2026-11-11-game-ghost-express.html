Ghost Express

Released 2026-11-11

"Ghost Express" is a 2D storage-organizing puzzle game about Spooky, a ghost who's too cute to scare anyone. Pack each shop's goods one by one into the box grid, then deliver them to the apartment nearby. As goods pile up and boxes fill, can Spooky complete the challenge?
