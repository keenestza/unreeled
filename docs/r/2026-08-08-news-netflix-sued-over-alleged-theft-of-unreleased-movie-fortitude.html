Netflix Sued Over Alleged Theft of Unreleased Movie 'Fortitude'

Released 2026-08-08

Netflix has been sued by the producer of an unreleased movie called 'Fortitude' over the alleged theft of a hard drive containing the film. The producer, Simon Afram, is asking for $105 million in damages, claiming the theft has ruined the movie's exclusivity and impacted its marketability.
