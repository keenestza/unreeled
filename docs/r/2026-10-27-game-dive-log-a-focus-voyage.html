Dive Log: A Focus Voyage

Released 2026-10-27

Dive Log is an idle treasure-hunting game driven by a pomodoro timer: every stretch of focus becomes a deep-sea voyage. Collect treasures, upgrade your submarine and hunt the relic hidden in each sea. Play your own music and ambience, and design the sea's treasures to share with other captains.
